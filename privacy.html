Privacy Policy — Under the Cap Publisher

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Under the Cap Publisher is used only by the owner of the Under the Cap channel for their own TikTok account.

What is accessed

Basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and permission to upload videos as drafts (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload).

What is stored

The access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. No data about other TikTok users is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Deletion

Revoking access in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
